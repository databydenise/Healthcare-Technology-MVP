# Project Brief

## Sector
Healthcare Technology

## Problem Space
The project focuses on challenges people face when managing their personal
health, such as medication reminders, symptom tracking, and preparation for
remote healthcare consultations.

## Objective
Build a minimum viable product (MVP) that helps users take better control
of their health routines.

## Project Functions

- Product Management
- Product Design
- Data Analytics
- Digital Marketing
- Frontend Development
- Backend Development
